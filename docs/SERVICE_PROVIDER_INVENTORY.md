### Official product limits, kept separate from actual configuration

- **Supabase API/database logs:** the [current pricing table](https://supabase.com/pricing) lists **1 day** for Free, 7 days for Pro, 28 days for Team and 90 days for Enterprise. Free is the verified current organization plan, so 1 day is the applicable published API/database-log plan limit. This is not a measured oldest-event test or a deletion guarantee for every log, backup or database table. The [Logs Query usage page](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/logs-query) describes a future degraded-state reduction to 1 hour for Free, but explicitly says those consequences are **not yet enforced**; that future limit is not attributed to this project.
- **Supabase Auth audit storage:** [Auth Audit Logs](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/audit-logs) distinguishes external log storage from optional database storage in `auth.audit_log_entries`, controlled by **“Write audit logs to the database.”** The API/database plan limit does not establish this database copy's cleanup policy. No audit rows were queried. The [log-drains guide](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/observability/log-drains) limits the managed feature to paid plans; Free plan metadata alone is not an inspection of every possible export or prior external copy.
- **Vercel runtime logs:** [Runtime Logs](https://vercel.com/docs/logs/runtime) lists Hobby 1 hour, Pro 1 day and Enterprise 3 days, or 30 days with Observability Plus. The add-on is verified off, but the base plan is not exposed by the checked connector response, so no single runtime retention value is assigned to Room Rubato.
- **Vercel build logs and traces are different classes:** [platform limits](https://vercel.com/docs/limits#logs) say build logs are retained indefinitely per deployment. The [2026-11-06 tracing announcement](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-tracing-is-now-generally-available) separately gives trace retention of 3 days on Hobby and 14 days on Pro/Enterprise. It also describes automatic tracing for qualifying new Next.js 16.5 projects. Neither the presence of tracing nor a qualifying-project status was established here; no trace payloads were read. Do not reuse the older trace tool description's runtime-log windows as current trace policy.
- **Provider operational/security/support information:** the [Vercel privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice) and [Supabase privacy notice](https://supabase.com/privacy) describe purpose/legal-need-based retention and their own controller scope, separately from customer-data processing. They do not establish a single project-specific retention period for every such record or prove that all processing occurs in Seoul. Their policies are not Room Rubato's own legal policy.
