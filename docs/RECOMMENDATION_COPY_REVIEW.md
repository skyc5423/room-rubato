# Recommendation copy calibration

Reviewed 2026-11-12. This is a focused category2/3 pass, not a claim that the whole catalogue or product claims have been validated. No personal responses were read for research. Changes are limited to domains-pillar1/2/3 source copy, with regression tests. Application rendering is handled separately.

## Evidence and editorial method

- Toss UX Writing principles: https://toss.tech/article/8-writing-principles-of-toss . Use predictable actions, concrete language and concise useful information. Do not equate brevity with omitting material limits.
- Samsung MX UX Writing guidance: https://design.samsung.com/kr/contents/ux-writing/ . Avoid generic benefit inflation; communicate actual capabilities and limits. Friendliness is not forced familiarity.
- AI-style caveat: https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.657/ . This English academic-corpus research shows vocabulary patterns changing as writers adapt. It does not validate an individual Korean UI 'AI detector'. This pass evaluates accuracy and usefulness, not authorship.

## Verified factual correction

Fritz official FAQ: https://fritz.co.kr/faq/list.html . Checked current text: Old Dog has low-toned acidity and dark-chocolate notes; Seoul Cinema has a lighter roast, bright acidity and blueberry notes. The prior combined recommendation claimed the products had no acidity. The replacement distinguishes the two blends and does not generalize their flavor claims to the separately named chocolate brands. Product formulations and descriptions can change; retain the review date and recheck when updating this recommendation.

## Financial boundary

Investor.gov: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation . Investment timeframe and risk tolerance matter to allocation. This application's image/scene choices do not establish investment suitability or the ability to hold an investment. Removed guaranteed long-term holding, safe-growth and guaranteed resale-value claims. The replacement describes a topic to study and the need to assess risk, timing and product-specific terms; it is not a personalized investment recommendation.

## Editorial calibrations, not newly proven outcomes

Removed invented 'twice the nuttiness' sensory effect; guaranteed fashion/coffee/movie success; perfect rest/travel rhythm/proportions/warranty; universal emotional relief; compulsory paper-first work and thirty-minute walks; ungrounded three-hour productivity prescription; guaranteed comfort while walking; and two work/relationship character judgments. Replacements retain concrete observations such as fit, material, cocoa percentage, roasting level, screenplay structure, frame joints, wall scale, route timing and equipment weight, without promising an outcome.

Twenty-five distinct copy fields changed: the ten priority replacements, seven specifically requested follow-up claims, and eight adjacent clear guarantees/prescriptions found in recommendation fields. A later refinement to one replacement keeps its wording appropriate to lamps, frames and furniture rather than just tables. Neutral scene/question descriptions were left unchanged.

## Remaining boundary

Many static deepAnalysis fields still claim that all listed scenes were selected. Matching a hand-written phenotype signature does not prove those exact choices; the delivered ResultEvidence component now replaces those strings in both result renderers with actual recorded-axis evidence (see RESULT_EVIDENCE.md). The static strings are not rendered as personal history. Other blanket brand superlatives and artist/product facts need their own source-specific pass. Do not describe this focused regression suite as catalogue-wide factual certification.
